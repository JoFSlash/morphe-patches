What the patches change

No ads in the feed

Promoted answers, sponsored posts and Google ads are hidden on every page, and requests to ad servers never leave the phone.

The app stops reporting on you

Quora's app logs how long you read, every tab you press and which experiments you are in. Those calls are dropped before they are sent.

Your clipboard stays yours

Quora's pages can ask the app for whatever you copied last. With the patch they get an empty answer; pasting with the keyboard still works.

Debugging switched off

The release app turns on WebView debugging on every page load, so any computer it is plugged into can read your session. The patch turns it off.

QWebView.loadUrlDisable WebView debugging
super.loadUrl(QWebViewController.CLEAR_URL);
WebView.setWebContentsDebuggingEnabled(true);
WebView.setWebContentsDebuggingEnabled(false);
}

Patches

All 33 patches

All of these are on by default when you patch. Nothing you see in the app changes.

33 patches,pick what you want

Add to Morphe

Privacy and tracking: On by default. They stop Quora collecting more than it needs, and change nothing you see.

  • On by defaultRemove adsHides promoted answers, sponsored posts and Google ads in the feed and on question pages, and blocks requests to ad servers.On the page
  • On by defaultDisable analyticsTurns off Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Firebase Performance and Facebook app events, and their advertising ID collection.In the manifest
  • On by defaultBlock web trackersBlocks requests from Quora pages to third-party analytics and tracking services.On the page
  • On by defaultBlock app telemetryStops the app from sending usage logs to Quora, such as reading time, tab presses, experiments, app speed and errors.In the app
  • On by defaultBlock tracking logsStops Quora's pages from passing performance and sign-up tracking events to the app.In the app
  • On by defaultDisable tracking jobsStops the background jobs that tell Quora every hour whether your phone is charging and every day whether notifications are on.In the app
  • On by defaultBlock advertising IDStops the app from sending your advertising ID and Android ID to Quora.In the app
  • On by defaultBlock clipboard readingStops Quora's pages from reading what you copied. Pasting with the keyboard still works.In the app
  • On by defaultDisable WebView debuggingStops the app from letting a connected computer inspect its pages, which would show your account's cookies.In the app
  • On by defaultClean shared linksRemoves tracking parameters from Quora links you copy or share.In the app
  • On by defaultSpoof installerReports Google Play as the app installer, so Quora does not treat the patched app as installed from an unknown source.In the app

Feed and answers: Off by default, because they change what Quora shows you. Tick the ones you want.

  • OptionalHide Quora+ answersHides answers and posts that can only be read with a Quora+ subscription.On the page
  • OptionalHide Quora+ promotionsHides buttons that ask you to try or join Quora+.On the page
  • OptionalHide AI answersHides answers written by Poe bots.On the page
  • OptionalHide PoeHides links and buttons that open Poe or Quora's AI assistant.On the page
  • OptionalHide related questionsHides the related questions lists on question pages.On the page
  • OptionalHide suggested questionsHides the questions for you to answer in the feed.On the page
  • OptionalHide Spaces suggestionsHides the Spaces you might like suggestions in the feed.On the page
  • OptionalHide countsHides upvote, view and share counts on answers and posts.On the page
  • OptionalHide downvote buttonHides the downvote button on answers and posts.On the page
  • OptionalDisable video autoplayVideos in the feed and in answers only play when you tap them.In the app
  • OptionalEnable text selectionLets you select and copy text on every page.On the page
  • OptionalText sizeMakes the text on every page larger or smaller. You pick the size in the patch options.In the app

The app itself: The three prompt patches are on by default. The rest is up to you, and some have options.

  • On by defaultDisable update promptsStops the app from showing the Google Play update screen.In the app
  • On by defaultDisable rating promptsStops the app from asking you to rate it on Google Play.In the app
  • On by defaultDisable notification promptStops the app from asking for permission to show notifications. You can still turn them on in Android's settings.In the app
  • OptionalDisable sign-in promptsStops Google's "Save password?" and automatic Google sign-in prompts. The login page buttons still work.In the app
  • OptionalHide tabsRemoves the tabs you pick from the bottom bar. Home always stays.In the app
  • OptionalStart tabOpens the app on the tab you pick instead of Home.In the app
  • OptionalHide badgesHides the red dots and counts on the bottom bar tabs.In the app
  • OptionalOpen links in browserOpens links to other websites in your default browser instead of an in-app browser tab.In the app
  • OptionalDisable vibrationStops the app from vibrating when you tap buttons such as upvote.In the app
  • OptionalDisable tab prefetchingSaves data by loading the other tabs only when you open them.In the app
